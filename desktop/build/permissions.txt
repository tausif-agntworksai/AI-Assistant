WHAT JARVIS WILL USE ON THIS COMPUTER
=====================================

Jarvis is a voice assistant that runs on this machine. Before it does anything,
it asks you — once, on first launch — which of the following it may use. Every
one of them can be switched off then or changed later from the tray icon, and
the ones you switch off are refused by the engine itself, not merely hidden.

This page lists them so you know what you are installing before you install it.


THE MICROPHONE, AND WHAT HAPPENS TO WHAT YOU SAY
------------------------------------------------

Jarvis listens for the wake word "hey jarvis". Until that wake word fires,
audio stays in a small rolling buffer in memory on this computer and is scored
by a local model. Nothing is transcribed, nothing is written to disk, and
nothing is sent anywhere.

After the wake word, speech is transcribed by a speech model that also runs on
this computer. Commands the built-in rules recognise ("chrome kholo", "volume
50") never leave the machine at all. Only phrasings the rules do not recognise
are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to be understood, along with the time, the
title of the focused window and the battery level. Weather, news and the
neural voices also use the internet. All of this is the "Internet" permission,
and it can be switched off.


WHAT IT CAN DO TO THE COMPUTER
------------------------------

  Speakers              Answers out loud, in Hindi or English.
  Internet              Claude, weather, news, and the neural voices.
  Apps                  Opens, closes and switches between installed apps.
  Windows               Minimises, maximises, snaps and closes windows.
  Device readings       Battery, CPU, memory, disk and network state.
  Windows settings      Brightness, night light, and Settings pages.
  System commands       Runs PowerShell in the background, with no visible
                        window. Windows exposes brightness, wi-fi and the
                        Store-app list no other way.
  Your files            Opens your personal folders and searches them by name.
  Screenshots           Captures the screen to your Pictures folder.
  Clipboard             Reads what you have copied, and writes to it.  (off by default)
  Typing for you        Types into whatever window is focused.          (off by default)
  Power controls        Lock, sleep, sign out, restart, shut down.
  Messaging             Opens WhatsApp or email with a message prepared. Jarvis
                        never presses send.                              (off by default)
  Camera                Not used by anything today. Listed so that if a camera
                        feature is ever added it arrives switched off.   (off by default)


ANYTHING CONSEQUENTIAL ASKS FIRST
---------------------------------

Separately from the permissions above, every action is classified by how much
damage it could do if Jarvis misheard you:

  Opening an app, changing the volume, answering a question   runs immediately
  Sleep, lock, closing an app or window, typing               asks out loud
  Shutdown, restart, sign out, emptying the Recycle Bin,      always asks
  sending a message

Anything that is not a clear "yes" counts as a no. Shutdown and restart run on
a 15-second delay, so "cancel shutdown" stops one. Messaging never sends by
itself: it opens the chat with the message typed and waits for you.

Every action Jarvis takes — allowed, refused or failed — is written to an audit
log you can read in the app's Activity tab.


SIGNING IN
----------

If this build is configured with a Firebase project, Jarvis will not open the
microphone at all until you sign in with a verified email address that an
administrator has approved. The account is stored encrypted with your Windows
account's own key, and can be signed out from the tray icon at any time.


UNINSTALLING
------------

Everything Jarvis writes lives in one folder: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Jarvis. Deleting
it removes the speech models, the logs, your reminders and notes, and the
permission record.
